How the Show Works
① DEAL

The contestant is dealt 6 bounties, balanced across levels 1–3. That is the whole run — six bounties, six games.

② PICK

Before each game they choose one bounty from what's left of the pool and lock it in. The pool shrinks by one every game.

③ PLAY

They play the game for the pot on the board. When it's over the host calls it: WIN or FAIL.

④ THE POT

The pot starts at $10. Every failed bounty adds $1 to the next one. Wins are banked and the running total carries to the end of the show.
